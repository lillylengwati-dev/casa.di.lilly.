The Story

Soul Meets Silicon

Casa di Lilly is a Johannesburg-based marketing and AI automation agency that helps businesses grow by improving their online presence and automating customer communication.

"I started Casa di Lilly after witnessing countless talented business owners struggle with the same challenge — they had incredible products and services, but no one could find them online.

While working in digital marketing, I saw businesses spending fortunes on agencies that delivered cookie-cutter solutions. I knew there had to be a better way — premium quality without the premium price tag, personal attention without the corporate red tape.

Today, Casa di Lilly bridges the gap between human creativity and AI-powered efficiency, helping businesses not just survive online, but thrive. Every website we build, every automation we deploy, every brand we design carries one mission: to make your business unforgettable."

Phetogo

Founder & Creative Director

We provide website design, digital marketing, AI-powered customer assistance, automated bookings, lead follow-ups, and review management systems to help businesses capture more leads and save time.
