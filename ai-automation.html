What we do

What is AI business automation?

AI business automation uses software, connected apps and carefully configured AI assistants to handle agreed, repeatable tasks. A system can capture an enquiry, collect details, update a CRM, arrange a booking or prepare a follow-up, while sending sensitive or unusual requests to a person.

Automation with human oversight
The business value

Make the customer journey easier to manage.

When customer information is spread across inboxes, messages, spreadsheets and calendars, important details can be difficult to track. Casa di Lilly maps the process first, then connects the tools and handovers needed for the agreed workflow.

  • Reduce repetitive administration
  • Keep enquiries and next steps visible
  • Make booking and follow-up processes clearer
  • Keep a person involved when judgement is needed

Questions & answers

AI automation & business systems FAQs

Understand what these systems do, when they may help and how a project starts.

How do I know which system my business needs?
Start with the task or problem that takes the most time. Think about where customer enquiries arrive, how leads and bookings are tracked, which tasks are repeated or copied between tools, and where delays or missed follow-ups happen. Casa di Lilly can review your process and recommend a practical starting point.
What can Casa di Lilly help automate?
Solutions can include customer enquiries and support, lead capture and sales follow-ups, CRM and customer onboarding, online bookings, quotations and invoice preparation, internal approvals, stock and job tracking, business dashboards, custom AI assistants and integrations between existing tools. The right combination depends on your business process.
Will AI replace my staff or customer service team?
The systems are designed to support agreed repetitive tasks, not remove the need for human judgement. Workflows can include review, escalation and manual takeover so a team member can handle sensitive, unusual or complex requests. Financial approvals and important business decisions should remain with authorised people.
Can you connect automation to software I already use?
Often, yes, where the platform supports a suitable integration, API or approved connection and the required access is available. Compatibility is checked during scoping before an integration is included in the agreed work.
Are these ready-made products or custom projects?
This catalogue describes the types of solutions Casa di Lilly can scope and provide; it does not mean every feature is a ready-made product. Some projects use supported tools and integrations, while others need custom workflows or software development. The recommended approach is confirmed after understanding your requirements.
How is a project scoped and quoted?
We first discuss the business problem, the current process, the tools involved, the number of workflows and users, data access, usage needs and any required approvals. We then define the deliverables, integrations, testing, handover and support so you can review the proposed scope before deciding.
Are third-party software and usage costs included?
Third-party subscriptions and usage—such as AI services, telephony, messaging, hosting and software licences—depend on the platforms selected and how much they are used. Any applicable external costs should be identified separately in the project proposal.
Is the interactive CasaOS demo connected to a real CRM?
No. The interactive demo is a front-end simulation containing fictional sample records. It lets visitors explore the kind of screens and workflows a configured system may include. It does not send messages, create live appointments, connect to a real CRM or store submitted information.
How do we get started?
Book a strategy call or email Casa di Lilly with a short description of the process you would like to improve. You do not need a technical specification. We will discuss the problem, review your current tools and identify what a suitable next step could look like.
