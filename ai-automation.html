What we do

What is AI business automation?

AI business automation uses software, connected apps and carefully configured AI assistants to handle agreed, repeatable tasks. A system can capture an enquiry, collect details, update a CRM, arrange a booking or prepare a follow-up, while sending sensitive or unusual requests to a person.

Automation with human oversight
The business value

Make the customer journey easier to manage.

When customer information is spread across inboxes, messages, spreadsheets and calendars, important details can be difficult to track. Casa di Lilly maps the process first, then connects the tools and handovers needed for the agreed workflow.

  • Reduce repetitive administration
  • Keep enquiries and next steps visible
  • Make booking and follow-up processes clearer
  • Keep a person involved when judgement is needed

Questions & answers

AI automation FAQs

Quick answers about AI automation, implementation, pricing and ongoing support.

What can Casa di Lilly automate for a business?
Casa di Lilly can configure defined workflows for website, email, Instagram and WhatsApp enquiries; lead capture and qualification; CRM updates; appointment booking; missed-call recovery; AI voice reception; follow-up reminders; review workflows; notifications and human handovers. The right solution depends on the business process and supported integrations.
Does AI automation replace my staff or customer service team?
The systems are designed to support agreed repetitive tasks, not remove the need for human judgement. Workflows can include review, escalation and manual takeover so a team member can handle sensitive, unusual or complex requests.
Are software subscriptions and AI usage included in the setup price?
No. Third-party costs such as AI usage, telephony, messaging, WhatsApp/Meta providers, hosting and other subscriptions are separate unless a proposal explicitly says otherwise. These costs depend on the platforms and usage selected for the project.
Can you connect automation to my existing CRM, website or calendar?
Often, yes, where the platform supports a suitable integration, API or approved connection and the required access is available. Compatibility is checked during scoping before the integration is confirmed in a proposal.
Are the prices fixed for every business?
The published USD prices apply to the defined implementation scopes described on this page. Additional accounts, integrations, departments, booking rules, features or major workflow changes are assessed and quoted separately.
Can I receive a quote in South African rand or British pounds?
Yes. The published service-sheet prices are displayed in USD. Businesses in South Africa and the United Kingdom can request a project quotation in ZAR or GBP. The final quote is based on the agreed scope rather than a simple currency conversion.
Is the interactive CasaOS demo connected to a real CRM?
No. The interactive demo is a front-end simulation containing fictional sample records. It lets visitors explore the kind of screens and workflows a configured system may include. It does not send messages, create appointments, connect to a CRM or store submitted information.
How do we get started?
Book a strategy call or email Casa di Lilly with a short description of the current process and the task you would like to improve. The next step is to review the requirement, confirm platform compatibility and define the implementation scope.
